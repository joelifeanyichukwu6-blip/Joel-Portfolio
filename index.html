FAQs

Which platforms do you work on?
Circle.so, Skool and Discord for the community itself, plus GoHighLevel, Zapier and Make for the automations around it.
How does a project start?
You send a short message about what you are building. I look at what exists, then give you a written plan in fix order before any build begins.
How long does a build take?
It depends on the size. An audit is quicker than a full build or a migration. I give you a timeline in writing after the audit, so you know what to expect.
Will I be able to run the community myself?
Yes, that is the goal. Every build ends with a walkthrough and a simple SOP, so you or your team can run it without me.
Do you manage communities after launch?
Yes, if you want it. I do daily community management on Skool, with posts, replies, recognition and reports. You can also take the handover and run it yourself.
Do you only work with coaches?
Mostly coaches, creators and brands, but I also build Discord servers for Web3 and sports communities, and I help with CRM and automation setups.
Can we talk before I decide?
Yes. You can book a 20 minute call from the button on this page, tell me what you are building and we can see if it is a fit.
How much does it cost?
It depends on scope. Tell me what you are building and I will reply with a plan and a quote. I do not guess prices before I understand the work.
Where are you based, and can we work across time zones?
I am in Lagos, Nigeria, and I work with clients worldwide. We agree on a reply window and a weekly check in at the start.
